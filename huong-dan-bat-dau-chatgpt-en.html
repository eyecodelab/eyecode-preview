Getting Started with ChatGPT for Everyday Work

Published Nov 9, 2026

Turn meeting notes into an action list and a clear email, then verify every factual detail before using the result.

Bắt đầu với ChatGPT cho công việc hằng ngày

Overview

For learners who want to complete this practical exercise with inspectable sample data and explicit checks.

  • Specify source material, a task, and an output format.
  • Check a summary and correct invented details.
  • Repeat a small workflow for everyday work.

You will build: A checked action table and a status email of at most 100 words.

Prerequisites

  • A browser and a ChatGPT account with access; no programming required.

Use ordinary text chat. Account limits vary; this exercise needs no particular paid plan or premium feature.

Step-by-step tutorial

Step 1. Prepare fictional meeting notes

Start a fresh chat so earlier context does not leak into the task. Copy the fictional notes below. For a workplace trial, replace real names with team labels.

TEXT
Monday planning
Design team: send two homepage mockups by Wednesday.
Engineering: review mockups after delivery.
Budget approval: no owner or deadline agreed.

Step 2. Request an evidence-based action table

PROMPT · INSTRUCTIONS
Using only the notes above, create a table: task | owner/team | deadline | evidence from the notes. Mark missing information as "Unspecified". Preserve weekday wording; do not invent calendar dates.

Inspect the budget row first. If the model invents an approver or deadline, ask it to remove the assumption and regenerate that row. Good formatting is not evidence of accuracy.

Step 3. Turn the table into an email

PROMPT · INSTRUCTIONS
Write a project-team update from the corrected table, at most 100 words. Include Design’s task, Engineering’s next step, and one question about the budget owner. Do not promise an unagreed completion date.

Step 4. Verify and save the result

Compare the email against all three source lines. Check that “review after delivery” has not become a new deadline. Use your editor’s word count when length matters. Copy the corrected text into a draft; choose recipients and send through your own email application.

Expected result
Design: two mockups by Wednesday.
Engineering: review after delivery.
Budget: owner and deadline unspecified.

Verification

  • No new person, date, or commitment appears beyond the notes.
  • The table marks unknowns, and the email stays within 100 words.

Troubleshooting

ChatGPT guesses deadlines

Cause: The prompt leaves the evidence boundary unclear.

Fix: Require source-only answers and evidence for each row.

The email is too long or has the wrong tone

Cause: Audience and length were unspecified.

Fix: Name the audience and word limit; request a style revision without new facts.

Exercises & extensions

  • Add a note with contradictory deadlines and ask the model to flag the conflict.
  • Continue to the prompting tutorial and build a reusable email checklist.

This is a drafting aid, not a fact-verification service. Use no personal or confidential material in the exercise; check your organization’s data policy before real use.
