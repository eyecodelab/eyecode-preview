ICLR 2026Learning theory

Transformers are Inherently Succinct

Official Outstanding Paper · Source

Studies how compactly Transformers represent formal languages. The proofs compare their representation size with automata and temporal logic, while exposing the computational difficulty of model verification.

ICLR 2026Language models

LLMs Get Lost In Multi-Turn Conversation

Official Outstanding Paper · Source

Examines failures when instructions arrive over multiple conversation turns. Simulated conversations separate task aptitude from reliability and trace how premature assumptions affect later responses.

ICLR 2026Optimization

The Polar Express: Optimal Matrix Sign Methods and their Application to the Muon Algorithm

Official Honorable Mention · Source

Computes the matrix polar decomposition used by Muon. Minimax-designed polynomial iterations use GPU-friendly matrix multiplications, with analysis of approximation error and finite-precision behavior.

ICLR 2025AI alignment & safety

Safety Alignment Should be Made More Than Just a Few Tokens Deep

Official Outstanding Paper · Source

Investigates safety alignment concentrated in the first output tokens. Case studies and regularized fine-tuning objectives explore deeper alignment and its persistence under subsequent fine-tuning.

ICLR 2025Data attribution

Data Shapley in One Training Run

Official Honorable Mention · Source

Attributes training data contributions to a particular model run. It accumulates Shapley values of gradient updates rather than repeatedly retraining models on different data subsets.

ICLR 2025Image & video segmentation

SAM 2: Segment Anything in Images and Videos

Official Honorable Mention · Source

Extends promptable segmentation from images to video. A Transformer with streaming memory works with an interactive data engine to maintain prompted object masks across frames.
