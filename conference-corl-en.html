CoRL 2025Robot control & manipulation

Learning a Unified Policy for Position and Force Control in Legged Loco-Manipulation

Official Best Paper · Source

Learns a unified position-and-force policy for legged robots. Simulated reinforcement learning uses state history to estimate forces and compensate motion, supporting contact-rich manipulation without force sensors.

CoRL 2025Robot control & manipulation

Fabrica: Dual-Arm Assembly of General Multi-Part Objects via Integrated Planning and Learning

Official Best Paper · Source

Combines hierarchical planning and learned control for dual-arm assembly. Assembly sequences, grasps, and motion plans connect with local contact policies across multi-part object geometries.

CoRL 2024Embodied learning

PoliFormer: Scaling On-Policy RL with Transformers Results in Masterful Navigators

Official Outstanding Paper · Source

Learns indoor navigation from RGB using large-scale simulated reinforcement learning. A vision encoder and causal Transformer combine perception with history for studying transfer to real robots.

CoRL 2024Robot control & manipulation

One Model to Drift Them All: Physics-Informed Conditional Diffusion Model for Driving at the Limits

Official Outstanding Paper · Source

Controls vehicles near handling limits using conditional diffusion. A physics-informed dynamics-parameter distribution is conditioned on online measurements and integrated into model predictive control.
