OmniRetarget: Interaction-Preserving Data Generation for Humanoid Whole-Body Loco-Manipulation and Scene Interaction

Official Best Paper · Source

Retargets human motion to humanoids while preserving body–object–terrain relationships. An interaction mesh and kinematic constraints generate and augment motion data for loco-manipulation.

ICRA 2025Uncertainty estimation

Marginalizing and Conditioning Gaussians onto Linear Approximations of Smooth Manifolds with Applications in Robotics

Official Best Paper · Source

Derives Gaussian marginalization and conditioning on general linear manifolds. Linearization extends the method to smooth manifolds, with covariance-estimation applications in SLAM and constrained optimization.

ICRA 2025Robot perception & memory

MAC-VO: Metrics-aware Covariance for Learning-based Stereo Visual Odometry

Official Best Paper · Source

Introduces metric-aware uncertainty to stereo visual odometry. Learned covariance guides keypoint selection and residual weighting, connecting image-matching reliability with pose-graph optimization.
